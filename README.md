# Spaced:  Learn things, forever.
   This is Spaced, my biggest attempt to make something really cool and actually useful with my coding     abilities. I found myself with an internshipless summer, and this is how I made the most of it. I
                    treated Spaced how a founder would treat a startup - I took investor meetings, I networked, and did
                    plenty of customer exploration. I have have an LLC and bank account for it! Ultimately, Spaced
                    reflects my position on how I can use coding to fulfill my purpose: bringing people something that
                    they can (and want to!) use to realize their potential.
                    I started Spaced during the schoolyear as an outlet to play around with new tech (Cursor) and had
                    the goal of solving one of my own problems: learning. Even though I'm taking a lot of cool classes,
                    I often feel that I don't retain nearly as much as I want to. Science has a pretty clear answer to
                    this: Spaced repetition and active recall put learning capabilities in overdrive, simultaneously
                    lowering the total studying time needed and boosting retention. One of the biggest issues I've seen
                    is that there is no online app to streamline this process (or at least one that I like). I
                    recognized that the power of LLMs is enabling chatbots to function at a level never before seen, and
                    saw a great opportunity: what would happen if we tied a spaced repetition algorithm together with a
                    tutoring chatbot that could intelligently create active recall questions and talk to users about
                    them? I thought this was gold, so I got to work.
                    Before this project, I had never done a lot of the things that were required of me. I'd never
                    interacted with AWS servers. I'd never set up routing for a website. I'd never even heard of a
                    "staging branch" before this. I can't even list all of the new things that I learned through
                    creating Spaced. As a result, it took a long time to do many things that probably shouldn't have
                    taken very long. Additionally, due to my often overzealous ideating, there were many features that I
                    put a lot of time into that realistically were doomed before they even started. The biggest example
                    of this is voice to voice interaction. It is an awesome idea and really take things to the next
                    level, but doing voice to voice is realllllly hard. To get the latency low enough you must need to
                    be a wizard or something. There are even pre-built alternatives like LiveKit, which I tried out, but
                    I couldn't even figure out that much. There have been many ups and downs, but I've put many hours
                    into Spaced and I'm happy where it's ended up.

Check it out at https://getspaced.app!
